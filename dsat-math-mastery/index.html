Official SAT Reference Sheet (Geometry & Trigonometry)

Circle Area & Circumference

$A = \pi r^2$

$C = 2\pi r$

Rectangle & Triangle Area

$A = \ell w$

$A = \frac{1}{2} b h$

Pythagorean Theorem

$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

Special Right Triangles

$45^\circ - 45^\circ - 90^\circ$: $x, x, x\sqrt{2}$

$30^\circ - 60^\circ - 90^\circ$: $x, x\sqrt{3}, 2x$

Rectangular Prism & Cylinder Volume

$V = \ell w h$

$V = \pi r^2 h$

Sphere, Cone, & Pyramid Volume

$V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$

$V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h$

$V = \frac{1}{3} B h$

Key Angle Properties

The number of degrees of arc in a circle is $360^\circ$.

The number of radians of arc in a circle is $2\pi$.

The sum of the measures in degrees of the angles of a triangle is $180^\circ$.

Master Tutor's Desmos Calculator Speed Hacks

1. Solving Equations with One Variable

Type the equation directly: 3x - 8 = 19. Desmos draws a vertical line at the exact $x$-solution. Click the $x$-intercept to read the value.

2. System Intersections & Tangency

Type both equations on separate lines: y = 2x^2 + 8x - 3 and y = -4x + k. Add a slider for k and adjust until they touch at exactly 1 point, or type the discriminant formula.

3. Circle Center & Radius

Desmos graphs implicit circle equations without completing the square: x^2 + y^2 - 10x + 6y + 9 = 0. Hover over the extrema points to calculate radius.

4. Exponential & Linear Regressions

Add a table ($x_1, y_1$), enter values, then type y1 ~ a*b^x1 or y1 ~ m*x1 + b. Desmos solves for parameters with $R^2$.

5. Fraction & Combinatorics

Click the [a/b] button to turn any decimal into an exact reduced fraction. Use nCr(n, r) and nPr(n, r) for counting.
