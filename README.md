# 2026 Digital SAT (DSAT) Math Field Notebook & Master Curriculum

A comprehensive, field-tested **Digital SAT (DSAT) Math Master Curriculum and interactive study application** specifically tailored to the **2026 College Board Bluebook™ Hard-Tier Module 2** standards.

Designed with an authentic **Master Tutor's Field Notebook and Textbook** aesthetic—complete with warm paper textures, classic red notebook margin rules, sticky-note marginalia, yellow/cyan highlighter accents, a personal scratchpad, and print-ready chapter formatting.

---

## 📚 Curriculum Structure

The curriculum covers all 9 essential high-frequency domains on the 2026 DSAT:

1. **Probability and combinatorics** (conditional probability $P(A \mid B)$, restricted sample spaces in two-way tables, independent events, combinations).
2. **Nonlinear equations and tangency** (quadratic-linear systems, discriminant applications $\Delta = B^2 - 4AC = 0$, finding constants where a line is tangent to a parabola).
3. **Area and circumference of circles** (completing the square for $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$, radian $\leftrightarrow$ degree conversions, arc length $s = r\theta$, and sector area $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$).
4. **Two-variable exponential regression** (interpreting $y = a(b)^x$ and $y = a(b)^{t/k}$ in context, initial values, periodic compounding rates, and Desmos table regressions `y1 ~ a*b^x1`).
5. **Similar shapes: Area and volume ratios** (linear scale factors $k$, squaring for area ratios $k^2$, cubing for volume ratios $k^3$, and reverse scaling).
6. **Nonlinear systems of equations** (systems with quadratics, circles, or exponential models; finding number of intersections $0, 1, 2, 4$).
7. **Linear equations in two variables** (slope-intercept form, parallel/perpendicular slopes $m_1 m_2 = -1$, interpreting constants in context, infinite/no solution systems).
8. **Statistical studies, sampling bias, and generalization** (observational studies vs. randomized controlled experiments, random sampling vs. random assignment, margin of error interpretations).
9. **Rational expressions and equations** (domain restrictions, vertical asymptotes vs. holes, solving rational equations, and eliminating extraneous solutions).

## 🛠️ Features

- **Interactive Practice Engine**: Supports both Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) and Student-Produced Response (SPR Grid-Ins) with flexible validation (accepting both simplified fractions like `31/49`, `6/13`, `15/4` and decimals like `0.633`, `0.462`, `3.75`, as well as comma-separated integers like `20,600`).
- **Dual-Path Solutions**: Every exemplar problem features both a **Formal Step-by-Step Algebraic Derivation** and a **15-Second Desmos Speed Hack**.
- **Tutor's Sticky Notes**: Warnings and alerts detailing exact trap phrasing from the Bluebook question bank.
- **My Study Scratchpad**: A collapsible yellow legal notepad on the right margin with auto-save to browser storage.
- **Formula Reference Sheet**: Modal containing official DSAT Geometry & Trigonometry reference formulas.
- **Print / PDF Ready**: Clean `@media print` styling that formats the active chapter as a published textbook page.
